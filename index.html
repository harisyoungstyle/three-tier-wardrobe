Three Tiers of a Wardrobe: Basics, Benchmarks, and Icons

Every strong wardrobe is built in tiers. The bottom tier is what you wear daily, the middle tier is the standard you measure against, and the top tier is the one piece with a name.

The Daily Tier

Basics with honest fabric: plain tees, sweatshirts, and layers that survive constant washing. Ranges from Mixed Emotion sit in this tier, everyday pieces in neutral colours that carry the week without demanding attention.

The Benchmark Tier

The middle tier is the standard everyone else is judged by. The ripped denim and graphic fleece associated with Amiri play this role in modern streetwear, a reference point for fit and finish even when you are shopping smaller labels.

The Icon Tier

The top tier is one icon piece, worn constantly: strong silver, made to be lived in. The jewellery and apparel at Chrome Hearts occupy this tier, the permanent detail that turns daily basics into a recognisable style.

Basics for the week, a benchmark for your standards, one icon for your identity.
